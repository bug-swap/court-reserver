CU Court Reserver

This project schedules an EMS court reservation for two days ahead at 6–7 PM Mountain time. Squash is the default sport; badminton can be selected in repository variables.

The booking runs after midnight in Denver. A separate scheduled visit refreshes the EMS session. Confirmation and failure notices are sent by email.

Open CU EMS

The schedule and session are maintained by GitHub Actions. This page does not hold login or contact details.
